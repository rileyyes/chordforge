    1 Record your parts

    Record or upload guitar and vocal takes here. Each one becomes a part you can trim, adjust the volume of, and place into a section below.

    Pick an existing part above and it'll play back as you record, so you can perform in time with something you already laid down — e.g. hear your guitar while you sing. Use headphones: without them, the playback will bleed into your microphone.

    No parts recorded yet.

    2 Arrange your sections

    Add the sections your song needs (Intro, Verse, Chorus, ...) and tick which parts play in each one. A section's length is simply the length of the longest part placed in it — there's nothing to configure. Tick "loop" for a short part (like a guitar riff) that should repeat to fill the section.

    3 Build your song

    This assembles your sections in order and runs your recordings through a fixed mixing chain: levels balanced, the guitar ducked slightly under your vocals so they sit on top, light compression and EQ, smooth crossfades between sections, and a final limiter. Nothing is generated — only your own recordings, mixed.
